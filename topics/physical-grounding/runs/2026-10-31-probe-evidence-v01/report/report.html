How to read this

The 1 ms evidence audit independently checks recorded native simulator rows. The policy gate is sampled at control boundaries; the report preserves any disagreement. “Final planning gate” combines the simulation receipt with caller-declared load, friction, geometry, force-limit and pose assumptions. It is a planning result, not permission to actuate.

CPU values show per-attempt policy-call and complete-step computation summaries, in milliseconds (mean / p95). They exclude sensors, transport, physical actuation and evidence serialization. Host timing is not a real-time guarantee.

The evidence receipt uses privileged simulator state and an authored synthetic force ceiling. It does not measure real material properties, establish a calibrated force limit, or demonstrate that a physical gripper can hold the object.
