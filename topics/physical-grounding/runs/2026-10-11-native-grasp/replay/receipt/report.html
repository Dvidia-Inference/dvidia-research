What this advances

This environment provides a task and negative controls for a future small DVIDIA action policy. Footage review, robot actions, learned control and physical qualification remain separate stages. Rigid-block contact does not validate a soft shoe, laces or a real gripper.

Runtime and timing scope

Attempt wall time includes asset checks, compilation, reset, planning, stepping and trace capture; separate inner timings identify narrower boundaries. No rendering, sensing, training, disk serialization, transport or physical actuation.
