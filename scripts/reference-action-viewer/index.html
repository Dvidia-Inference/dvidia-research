All eight attempts complete their recorded control intervals, but neither candidate completes a strict placement: 0/4 absolute and 0/4 reference-relative. These small, reused development cases do not establish a population success rate or a comparison against the original fixed controller.

The learner sees causal camera estimates, explicit missing-value masks, joint measurements and previous applied references. Native object and contact truth is used only for independent scoring. There is no teacher fallback. The two earlier infrastructure batches contain sixteen refusals before learned control; they remain in the research report.

Capture and arrival timestamps are simulation clocks. Playback and inference speed do not establish hardware response time.
