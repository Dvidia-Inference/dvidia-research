# DVIDIA visual identity

The research site uses quiet, grainy white paper, dark ink, a left navigation
column and a narrow serif reading measure. Scholarly titles, abstracts, section
anchors, tables and dated research footprints keep evidence readable. The local
paper texture is a tiny SVG noise asset, not a scientific figure. Existing
editorial illustrations retain their separate [provenance](PROMPTS.md), but are
not used as publication covers in the reader.

The two unchanged, locally served IBM Plex font files are licensed under SIL
OFL 1.1. Copyright and license notices accompany them in `fonts/`. Sans supplies
weights 100–700; Mono supplies regular 400. Both use `font-display: swap` and
system fallbacks. The article body uses local system serif fonts. No external
font service or font request is required.

Primary font source: [IBM Plex](https://github.com/IBM/plex).
The DVIDIA mark is platform identity, not a research result or certification.
