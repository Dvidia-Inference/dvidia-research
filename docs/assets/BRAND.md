# DVIDIA visual identity

The research site uses the platform's established design language: IBM Plex Sans
and Mono, paper `#f7f6f2`, ink `#111111`, voltage blue `#3c6cff`, the eight-bar
visor mark and rounded blue buttons. The editorial illustrations retain their
separate [provenance](PROMPTS.md).

The two unchanged, locally served IBM Plex font files are licensed under SIL
OFL 1.1. Copyright and license notices accompany them in `fonts/`. Sans supplies
weights 100–700; Mono supplies regular 400. Both use `font-display: swap` and
system fallbacks. No external font service or font request is required.

Primary font source: [IBM Plex](https://github.com/IBM/plex).
The DVIDIA mark is platform identity, not a research result or certification.
