What changed

Read the boundaries

One simulated rigid 40 g block, two known goals, and a narrow starting envelope. Privileged simulator contacts and state, rather than cameras or human videos. The acquisition latch does not continuously certify retention. There is no hardware, real-time, or general video-to-skill claim.

The compact bundle omits native arrays, complete traces, and the full raw report. Those remain retained locally and are identified by hashes. This publication does not upload that raw archive.
